Spot and Futures signals in a single app

CPE Trade Pro covers both crypto markets in one place. Spot is long-only, Futures supports shorting, and the same BUY / SELL / HOLD signals map onto whichever market you choose to trade.

7 min read Updated 2026 Category Crypto signals

Spot and Futures are not the same game, and treating them as one is a common way to lose money. CPE Trade Pro keeps them separate — one app analyses both markets, but each signal knows which market it belongs to and what that market can actually do.

Spot is the straightforward market. You buy an asset and hold it; you own the coin. That makes Spot long-only, which has one important consequence: a SELL setup does not translate into a Spot position. On Spot there is nothing to short, so a bearish setup only applies if you are running a suitable Futures position or a supported Forex workflow.

Futures trade a contract on the price, including perpetual contracts that never expire. Because you can be long or short, both BUY and SELL setups have a path to execution. That flexibility comes with leverage, and leverage cuts both ways — it can magnify gains and magnify losses just as quickly.

The analysis underneath is the same for both. The app runs automatic multi-factor analysis, forecast and future prediction lines, and the Smart Money Concepts chart overlay that draws order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity and BOS / CHoCH structure breaks. You read the chart the same way; only the market mechanics change.

When you are ready to act, Auto Trade can open trades for the signal grades you choose, while Manual Trade leaves the decision with you. Either way, DEMO follows each eligible signal with a fixed $10 paper stake and places no real orders, so you can watch both markets behave before risking anything.

Common mistakes when mixing the two

Because both markets live in one app, it is easy to lose track of which one a signal belongs to. A few habits keep the two separate:

  • Treating a SELL signal as a Spot trade. On Spot there is nothing to short; route it to Futures or a supported Forex workflow instead.
  • Using Futures leverage to win back a loss. Bigger size does not fix a bad plan — it just shortens the path to liquidation.
  • Ignoring funding on perpetuals. A position held for a long time pays or earns funding, which changes the true cost of the trade.
  • Forgetting which market you acted in. Spot and Futures positions carry different risk, so keep them apart in your own notes.
  • Skipping DEMO. The $10 paper stake exists so you can learn the mechanics before real money is involved.

FAQ

Why can't I use a SELL signal on Spot?

Spot is long-only — you buy and hold the asset, so there is nothing to short. A SELL setup applies only to a suitable Futures position or a supported Forex workflow.

Do Spot and Futures use different analysis?

No. Both use the same automatic multi-factor analysis and the same Smart Money Concepts overlay. What changes is the market mechanics, not the chart logic.

Can Auto Trade run on both markets?

Auto Trade opens trades for the signal grades you choose. Real orders are placed only in LIVE mode with your own connected platform credentials; in DEMO everything stays paper-only.

Is Futures riskier than Spot?

Futures allows shorting and leverage, and liquidation is possible if margin is exhausted. Spot has no leverage and your risk is limited to what you paid for the asset. Both still carry risk.
