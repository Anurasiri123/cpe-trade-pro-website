Refund policy

Try the app free for 7 days first. If you then buy a licence and something is wrong, you can request a refund within 14 days of purchase.

Last updated 2026 Applies to licence purchases

1. Try before you buy

CPE Trade Pro includes a free 7-day trial. We encourage you to use the full trial to confirm the app runs on your device and does what you need. If the trial does not convince you, simply do not buy a licence — no payment is taken and nothing is owed.

2. Refund window

If you purchase a licence and want a refund, contact us within 14 days of the purchase date. Requests made after this window may not be accepted.

3. When we will refund

  • The app cannot run on your device and our support cannot make it work.
  • You were charged twice, or the charge was an obvious mistake (for example a duplicate or accidental purchase).
  • You bought a licence but cannot activate it, and we cannot issue a working licence.
  • Where the law in your country gives you a stronger right, that right always applies.

4. When we may not refund

  • You have been using the licensed app normally and simply changed your mind after the trial already let you test it.
  • You are requesting a refund because a trade lost money. The software is an analysis tool; trading results are never guaranteed and are not a basis for a refund.
  • The request is outside the 14-day window.
ℹ️
We do not gate your money We never hold funds and never ask for a fee to "release" a withdrawal. Refunds are returned to the same payment method you used to pay us — nothing more is ever requested from you.

  1. Message us on WhatsApp +94 77 494 0944 or email Anurasiri123@gmail.com.
  2. Include your order details (date, amount, and the email or name used to pay) and a short description of the problem.
  3. If the issue is technical, we may first try to fix it with you. If we cannot, we proceed with the refund.

6. Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it can take a few business days to appear. We will confirm in writing once the refund has been issued.

7. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please contact us first — we can almost always resolve it faster than a bank dispute. We will always act in good faith and in line with this policy and your statutory rights.
