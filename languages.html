One app, your language

CPE Trade Pro is built for traders around the world, so the interface does not force one language on you. Pick the language you are most comfortable with and the app's menus, signal labels, controls and learning content follow it.

Choose from English, Sinhala, Tamil, Hindi, Chinese, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Indonesian or Thai — that is 15 languages in total. Arabic switches the layout to a right-to-left reading direction.

🌐

Change it anytime Open settings inside the app and pick a different language. Your choice applies straight away — no reinstall and no separate download.

What stays the same

Brand names, exchange names and symbol tickers are kept in their original form so nothing is misread. Only the interface text is translated — the product name CPE Trade Pro is never renamed.

Every language shows the same signals, the same 0–10 quality score and the same LIVE / DEMO framing. A different language never changes what a signal means.

Good to know

  • The app is analysis software — not a broker, exchange or fund manager.
  • Nothing about the language you choose changes trading risk.
  • The 0–10 score is a quality score built from five measurable factors — not a win rate, a probability of profit, or an audited performance record.
