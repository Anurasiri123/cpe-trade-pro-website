Same engine, same overlay

Nothing about the analysis changes when you move from crypto to FX. The app applies the same multi-factor engine — trend and structure, momentum, volatility bands and a multi-timeframe check — and the same Smart Money Concepts overlay:

  • OB — order blocks, drawn as demand or supply zones.
  • FVG — fair value gaps left behind by fast moves.
  • LIQ — liquidity lines above equal highs or below equal lows.
  • BOS ↑ / BOS ↓ and CHoCH ↑ / CHoCH ↓ — the latest structure breaks.
  • LIMIT ENTRY · OB or MARKET ENTRY · FVG — the zone the current signal is built on.

Because the labels are the same across both asset classes, a trader who has learned the overlay on crypto can read an FX chart straight away. If the terms are new, start with our explainer on Smart Money Concepts.

Risk and leverage warnings

This is the part that deserves the most attention. Forex and metals are commonly traded with leverage, which can magnify losses as well as gains. A small adverse move can wipe out a large share of an account.

  • Always define the stop before the entry, and size the position so the stop is affordable.
  • Treat leverage as a risk multiplier, never as a way to buy confidence.
  • Practise in DEMO — it places no real orders and uses a $10 paper stake per eligible signal.
