INSIDE THE APP

The screens you actually work in

Analysis leads to a plan, the plan becomes a trade, and every trade stays reviewable. All of these are built into CPE Trade Pro.

🔍Analysis

Market Analysis & Auto Scan

Scan Market reviews a chosen universe and timeframe, Analyze Chart studies a single symbol, and Auto Scan (every 15m) keeps checking on its own. A scan log shows why each setup did or did not open a trade.

📈Chart Lab

Charts & Prediction Lines

The chart shows patterns and indicators, and can project into Future space: Signal targets from entry to TP and a Forecast 3M path. A Forecast tool also lets you draw your own projected line.

🖐️Manual

Manual Trade

Trade Now sends your own order — BUY or SELL, MARKET or LIMIT — with TP1 / TP2 / stop-loss, an optional trail and money management. Choose Start Live Trade or Start Paper Trade.

🤖Automated

Auto Trade Sessions

Start one or more sessions that trade Caution, Strong or Higher-Strong signals for you, with money management, a martingale ladder, auto take-profit and auto trail TP + SL. Stop All ends every session.

📋Positions

Positions & TP

Pending, open and closed positions are listed per market, with Live and Demo kept apart. Prices are checked every 15s and you can edit TP or partially close 25 / 50 / 75 / 100%.

🕘History

Signal History

Every BUY / SELL signal the software gives is tracked as if $10 was placed. Click a row to open its chart at the start and at the end, and see the profit or loss from that $10.

📰News

News & Tricks

Crypto news, a Forex tab and All platforms tips live in the app, with a refresh button so headlines stay current.

Auto Trade places real orders only in LIVE mode with your own connected platform credentials. In DEMO it stays paper-only.
