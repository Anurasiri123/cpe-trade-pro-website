About

Independent trading-analysis software

CPE Trade Pro is a desktop and mobile app that analyses crypto and Forex markets and shows BUY / SELL / HOLD signals with the evidence behind them. It is a software product — not a broker, not an exchange, and not a fund manager.

What CPE Trade Pro is

CPE Trade Pro is analysis software you install on your own device. It reads market data from the exchanges and brokers you choose, runs automatic multi-factor analysis (Bollinger Bands, Fibonacci, candlesticks, RSI, MACD, momentum, Smart Money Concepts and multi-timeframe checks), and presents a signal with an entry, stop, targets and a 0-10 quality score that is built from five measurable factors.

You decide what to do with a signal. The app can follow signals for you in DEMO with a fixed $10 paper stake, or place real orders in LIVE only after you connect your own platform credentials. Everything happens on your machine, with your accounts.

What CPE Trade Pro is not

This matters more than anything else on this page, so it is stated plainly:

  • We are not a broker or exchange. We do not hold, custody or manage your money at any point.
  • We never take deposits. There is no wallet to fund, no balance to top up.
  • We never ask you to pay a fee to withdraw. If anyone claiming to be us asks for a withdrawal fee, tax or "unlock" payment, it is a scam — do not pay it.
  • We never ask for your exchange password or API secret in a chat. Your credentials are entered in the app, on your device, and are stored locally.
  • We are not affiliated with any trading platform. CPE Trade Pro is not connected to, endorsed by, or a clone of cTrader, CPT Markets, Binance, Coinbase or any other brand named on this site. Those names are listed only to describe compatibility.

Our money rule We earn from software licences only. We do not earn from your trades, we do not take a cut of your profits, and we do not profit when you lose. There is no financial incentive for us to push you to trade.

How the software makes money

CPE Trade Pro is a paid app with a free 7-day trial. After the trial, you buy a licence, which is device-bound to the machine you activate it on. That licence fee is our only revenue. There are no deposit requirements, no performance fees, no revenue share and no referral payments from brokers.

The principles we build to

  • Show the evidence, not just the verdict. Every signal carries its 0-10 score breakdown and the chart reasons behind it, so you can judge it yourself.
  • Practise before you risk. DEMO with a paper stake exists so you can learn the mechanics with no real money on the line.
  • Keep your keys yours. Your exchange or broker API keys are entered by you, stored on your device, and used only to talk to the platform you connected.
  • No promises. The quality score describes measured evidence. It is not a probability of profit and not a guarantee. Markets can and do go against any setup.
  • Say it plainly. If something about how the app works is not clear, ask us and we will explain it in writing.
