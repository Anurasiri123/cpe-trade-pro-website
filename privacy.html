Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data CPE Trade Pro collects, why, and where it lives. The short version: your API keys and trading data stay on your own device, and we collect as little as possible.

Last updated 2026 Applies to cpetradepro.com and the app

The short version

  • Your exchange and broker API keys are entered in the app and stored on your own device. We do not ask you to send them to us.
  • Your settings, watchlists, signals, open trades and journal are stored locally on your device.
  • This website uses Google Analytics to understand anonymous traffic patterns.
  • We only hold the personal details you choose to send us when you contact us or buy a licence.

1. Data in the application

CPE Trade Pro is a local application. It stores its configuration and operating data in your operating system's user-data folder for the app — including your settings, watchlists, signal history, open trades and journal, and the API keys you enter for the platforms you connect. That data is used to run the app on your machine and to talk to the exchange or broker you connected.

API keys. You create your API credentials in your own exchange or broker account and enter them in the app. We do not operate a service that receives your keys, and we never ask you to send a key over WhatsApp, email or any chat. If anyone claiming to be us asks for your API secret, it is a scam.

Licence activation. Activating a licence may send your licence key and a device identifier to validate that the licence is genuine and bound to your device. This is used for licence management and to prevent abuse, not for marketing.

2. Data on this website

When you visit cpetradepro.com we may collect:

  • Usage analytics (pages viewed, approximate region, device and browser type, referring site) through Google Analytics, delivered via Google Tag Manager.
  • Interaction events such as clicks on the WhatsApp, email or download buttons, so we can tell which parts of the site are useful.
  • Standard server logs from our hosting provider and CDN.

We do not use this data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

3. Data you send us

If you contact us on WhatsApp, by email or through GitHub, we receive whatever you choose to send — for example your name, contact details, order information and any screenshots. We use it only to answer your query and to provide support, licences and refunds.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses cookies and similar storage set by Google Analytics to measure traffic. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work. The application itself does not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep data

Support messages and licence records are kept only as long as needed to provide support, meet legal or accounting obligations, and prevent fraud. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics settings. Data stored by the app on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete the app's local data from your device at any time.
  • You can block cookies and analytics in your browser.
  • You can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us.
  • You can stop using the software at any time.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect the data we hold, and the website is served over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your own device and API keys safe, restrict your API key permissions (for example, disable withdrawals on the key), and never share your keys with anyone.

9. Children

The software is intended for adults who are able to trade the relevant markets lawfully. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. International users

We support users in many countries. Where your data is processed by third-party providers such as Google or GitHub, it may be handled in other countries under their own safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page with an updated date.
