FEATURES

Everything a trader needs, in one terminal

No more jumping between apps. Signals, charts, patterns and learning — all inside CPE Trade Pro.

Signals BUY / SELL / HOLD Score 0–10, five factors Modes LIVE / DEMO
⚡

Live Buy / Sell Signals

BUY, SELL and HOLD setups with entry, stop-loss, take-profit levels, a 0–10 quality score and a readable breakdown.

🎯

Auto Analysis

Scan Market checks a chosen universe, Analyze Chart studies one symbol, and Auto Scan (every 15m) keeps running in the background. Reasons are shown, not hidden.

🔮

Future Prediction Lines

The chart can draw projections into Future space — Signal targets from entry to TP and a Forecast 3M path. You can also draw your own forecast line manually.

🖐️

Manual Trade

Trade Now places your own BUY / SELL with MARKET or LIMIT orders, TP1 / TP2 / stop-loss, trailing and optional money management — as a Live trade or a Paper trade.

🤖

Auto Trade

Run one or more Auto Trade sessions that open trades for Caution, Strong or Higher-Strong signals, with money management, a martingale ladder, auto take-profit and auto trail TP + SL.

📋

Positions & TP

A Positions / TP tab keeps pending, open and closed positions apart for Live and Demo. Prices are checked every 15s and you can edit TP or close 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% anytime.

⚡

Scalp Signals

Shorter-timeframe setups for active traders. Check spread, volatility and the stop distance before considering an entry.

📈

Long-Term Signals

Use higher-timeframe context to assess broader direction and avoid treating every short-term move as a trend change.

🔁

Multi-Timeframe

Compare selected timeframes side by side. A higher-timeframe confirmation can add context, but it cannot remove market risk.

🔗

Market Connections

Use the app's available exchange and broker data connections. Live order support depends on the selected platform; other connections remain analysis or paper-only.

🛡️

DEMO & LIVE Modes

DEMO mode uses paper trades and does not send real orders. LIVE mode requires your own supported platform credentials; trading still carries risk.

🔍

Auto Market Scan

Choose a scan universe and timeframe, then run a scan or enable the 15-minute auto-scan. A scan can still miss fast market moves.

🆕

Upcoming Listings & Alerts

Follow Binance-announced Spot and Futures listings, sorted by scheduled opening with a countdown. Recently listed pairs can show available signal context. While the app is running with notifications enabled, it can alert you when an announced listing is detected as live. If no exact time is announced, the app labels its estimate. New listings can be highly volatile and illiquid; an alert is not a buy signal, and losses are possible.

📓

Trade Journal

Log every trade, track win rate and review your performance to improve over time.

🕘

Signal History

Eligible BUY / SELL signals are tracked on a fixed $10 paper stake. Review open P&L, closed outcomes, fees and the start/end chart in Signal History.

📰

News & Tricks

Market news and pro trading tips delivered inside the app to keep you one step ahead.

🧠

Signal Quality & Trade Plan

See a five-part quality breakdown, entry, stop, targets and risk/reward context. The score describes measured evidence — it is not a guarantee.

📐

Chart & Candlestick Patterns

The app detects classic chart shapes — Double Top/Bottom, Triangles, Wedges, Ascending/Descending Triangles, Rounding Tops/Bottoms — and candlestick patterns such as Hammer, Engulfing, Doji, Morning/Evening Star and more, drawn and labelled on the chart automatically.
