Risk disclosure

Trading crypto and Forex carries a real risk of losing money. This page explains the risks in plain language so you can decide for yourself whether to trade at all.

Last updated 2026 Read before you trade
⚠️
You can lose money — including money you did not want to lose Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose. Never borrow money to trade. Nothing in this app removes trading risk.

1. This is not financial advice

CPE Trade Pro is analysis software. It does not know your income, your savings, your experience or your goals, and it cannot tell you whether a trade is suitable for you. Signals, scores and forecasts are general information, not personalised advice. If you need advice, speak to a licensed financial adviser in your country.

2. Trading risk

Prices move against positions all the time. A trade that looks strong can still lose. Markets can gap over your stop-loss, liquidity can disappear, and in fast conditions your order may fill at a worse price than expected. No software can protect you from these market realities.

3. Leverage and liquidation

Futures and leveraged Forex trading multiply both gains and losses. With leverage, a relatively small adverse move can wipe out your margin and lead to liquidation — the forced closing of your position. In some cases losses can exceed the margin you deposited. If you do not fully understand leverage, do not use it.

4. What the 0-10 quality score is — and is not

Each signal carries a 0-10 quality score built from measurable factors, along with a breakdown of those factors. The score describes the evidence behind a setup at the moment it was generated. It is not:

  • a probability of profit;
  • a guarantee that a trade will win;
  • a measure of how much you should stake; or
  • a substitute for your own judgement and risk plan.

A high score and a winning trade are not the same thing. Use the score as one input, never as a promise.

5. Past results

Signal history and DEMO outcomes describe what happened in the past under the conditions the app recorded. They are not a forecast. Past performance never guarantees future results, and a system that worked in one market phase can fail in the next.

6. DEMO results are not real

DEMO and paper trading place no real orders. Simulated results do not include real-world effects such as slippage, partial fills, funding costs or changing liquidity, and they can look better than live results would. Use DEMO to learn the mechanics, not to predict profits.

7. Automated trading risk

Auto Trade executes according to rules you set and can open or close positions on your behalf. Automation does not remove risk: settings can be wrong, connectivity can fail, and an automated position can lose money while you are not watching. Only enable Auto Trade with a platform and an account you fully understand.

8. Platform and data risk

Market data comes from third-party exchanges and brokers and may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Those platforms can go down, change their rules, restrict your account or limit withdrawals under their own terms. We do not control them and are not responsible for their actions.

9. Security and access risk

You are responsible for keeping your device and your exchange API keys secure. Restrict key permissions (for example, disable withdrawals on the key), and never share your keys with anyone. No legitimate support person will ever ask for your API secret.

10. Avoiding scams

Be cautious of anyone who promises guaranteed returns, a fixed profit per trade, or urgent "limited-time" opportunities, or who asks you to pay a fee to release a withdrawal. These are classic fraud patterns. Real firms do not need you to pay to get your own money.

11. Your responsibility

Every trading decision is yours. Before you trade, decide what you are willing to lose, size positions so a normal losing run cannot end your account, and consider practising in DEMO first. If trading is causing you stress or losses you cannot absorb, stop and seek help.
