Security: what we will never ask you to do

If you receive a message that claims to be from CPE Trade Pro but asks for any of the following, it is a scam. Stop and report it to us.

⚠️
Red flags — real CPE Trade Pro never does these
  • Asks you to pay a "tax", "fee" or "commission" to unlock or release a withdrawal.
  • Asks you to deposit funds into an account we control. We do not have such an account.
  • Asks for your exchange or broker password, or your API secret, in a chat message.
  • Promises guaranteed daily or weekly returns, or a fixed profit per trade.
  • Pressures you to pay through an unusual route (crypto gift cards, personal wallets, informal transfers).
  • Asks you to install an app from a link that is not cpetradepro.com or our official GitHub releases page.

Report an impersonation

If someone is pretending to be CPE Trade Pro — on WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok or any other channel — please send us a screenshot with the phone number or account name. We will record it and warn other users. Knowing about these attempts helps everyone in our community.
