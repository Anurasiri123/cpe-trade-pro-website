How the signals work

Signals come from the app's automatic multi-factor analysis, which studies price across several timeframes and blends that with technical tools such as RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Fibonacci, candlesticks and momentum. The result is a clear direction — BUY, SELL or HOLD — shown alongside an entry zone drawn from the Smart Money Concepts overlay.

You can also let the app work on a schedule. The 15m auto-scan option refreshes the analysis at a regular interval, which suits a trader who cannot sit in front of a desk all day. Signals are kept in signal history, so you can review what was suggested and how it played out instead of relying on memory.
