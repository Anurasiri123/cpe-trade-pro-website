Signal grades: CAUTION, STRONG and HIGHER STRONG

Direction is only half of a signal. The other half is how much of the analysis actually agreed — and CPE Trade Pro expresses that as a grade. There are three live grades: CAUTION, STRONG and HIGHER STRONG. A read that reaches none of them stays a HOLD.

How the grade is calculated

Under the hood the app runs a set of independent checks on the chart and adds each one to a single directional score. A bullish check pushes the score up, a bearish check pushes it down, and how far it moves depends on the strength of that evidence. The checks include:

  • Band position — whether price sits at the Bollinger lower band (an oversold bounce) or the upper band (an overbought / take-profit zone).
  • Trend filter — where price sits relative to the SMA 50.
  • Momentum — 5-bar and 10-bar momentum, including volume spikes.
  • Smart Money structure — order blocks, fair value gaps, BOS and CHoCH.
  • Chart patterns — the strongest bullish or bearish pattern currently drawn on the chart.
  • Trend strength — ADX with +DI and −DI.
  • Oscillators — RSI, MACD, Money Flow Index, Williams %R and CCI.
  • Volatility context — how wide and how fast the market is moving right now.

This is not one indicator casting a vote. The grade only climbs when several different checks agree, and it only reaches the top tier when a second timeframe agrees too.

Nothing removes market risk, and even a top-grade signal can fail. You manage that with the stop loss and position size, not by trusting a label. The grade measures how much the evidence agreed — it is not a win rate, not a probability of profit and not an audited record.

The parts of a complete signal

Every actionable crypto signal is built from the same four levels. Together they form the plan:

  • Entry — the price the signal is based on, either at market or at a limit zone.
  • Stop loss (SL) — the level that says the idea is wrong. It defines your risk.
  • TP1 — the first target, where many traders take part of the position.
  • TP2 — the second target, for the remainder of the position.

Because the entry, the stop and the targets are all given, you can work out position size before you act, instead of after.

LevelWhat it answers
EntryWhere does the trade start?
Stop lossHow much am I risking if I am wrong?
TP1Where might I take a first piece off?
TP2Where is the fuller move expected to reach?
💡
Risk first, entry second The stop is the most important number in the signal. It tells you the idea is wrong before your account has to. Always know it before you size a position.

How the analysis engine decides

The engine is deliberately multi-factor. No single indicator gets the final vote. Instead the app layers several kinds of evidence and checks whether they agree:

📈 Trend and structure Market structure, order blocks, fair value gaps and liquidity shape where a move is likely to start.
⚡ Momentum Momentum, MACD and RSI help show whether a move is gathering strength or fading.
🎚️ Volatility bands Bollinger Bands and candlestick behaviour give context for how far a move might stretch.
🔁 Multi-timeframe check The setup is compared across timeframes, plus Fibonacci levels for confluence.

When the evidence lines up, you get a direction with levels. When it does not, you get a HOLD — which is the engine declining to invent an edge.

Crypto signals — quick answers

What exactly is a crypto signal?

It is a defined trade idea: a pair, a direction, an entry price, a stop loss and one or more targets. CPE Trade Pro adds the analysis behind each one so you can judge it rather than just follow it.

How many pairs are covered?

590+ combined crypto pairs, gathered from Binance Spot and USDT perpetual listings. The exact number changes as exchanges add and remove symbols.

Is HOLD a missed signal?

No. HOLD means the engine found no clean setup. Waiting is a decision, and often a profitable one, because it keeps you out of low-quality trades.

Can I short a coin on Spot?

Spot is long-only in the app. Shorting requires futures or a supported Forex workflow. Leverage can magnify losses.

Do the DEMO trades use real money?

No. DEMO uses a $10 paper stake per eligible signal and places no real orders. It is for practice on live market data.

What are the signal grades?

There are three: CAUTION, STRONG and HIGHER STRONG. The grade reflects how much of the analysis agreed — CAUTION is an early heads-up, STRONG is the normal tradable tier, and HIGHER STRONG is a STRONG signal that the higher timeframe also confirms.

How is the grade calculated?

The app runs a set of independent checks — band position, the SMA 50 trend filter, momentum, Smart Money structure, chart patterns, ADX with ±DI, oscillators such as RSI, MACD, MFI, Williams %R and CCI, and volatility context — and adds them into one directional score. The grade rises only when several different checks agree, and reaches the top tier only when a second timeframe agrees too.

Which grade is the best?

HIGHER STRONG is the top grade, because two timeframes agree. It is the strongest grade the app produces — but "strongest" is not "certain". Risk is still managed with the stop loss and position size, and the grade is not a win rate or a probability of profit.

What is the risk on each grade?

CAUTION carries the most risk because it is early and unconfirmed — it is never auto-traded. STRONG is the standard tier, where your risk is simply the entry-to-stop distance. HIGHER STRONG is the least uncertain of the three, but still not risk-free. Extreme volatility can downgrade a fresh STRONG to CAUTION, and market sentiment can soften a grade but never upgrade one.
