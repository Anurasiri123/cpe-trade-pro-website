BUY, SELL and HOLD

The three directions mean very different things, and it helps to treat them that way:

DirectionWhat it meansTypical use
BUYThe engine sees conditions favouring a move up from the entry.Look for a long entry at the planned level.
SELLConditions favour a move down. On Spot this is a signal to exit or stand aside; shorting needs Futures or a supported Forex workflow.Exit a long, or prepare a short where the market allows it.
HOLDNo clean edge was found, or the setup is not ready yet.Wait. Do not force a trade.
⚠️
A SELL signal is not always a short Spot trading is long-only in the app. A SELL on a Spot pair is a reason to reduce or exit, not to open a short. Short exposure requires a futures or supported Forex connection, and leverage can magnify losses.

The parts of a complete signal

Every actionable crypto signal is built from the same four levels. Together they form the plan:

  • Entry — the price the signal is based on, either at market or at a limit zone.
  • Stop loss (SL) — the level that says the idea is wrong. It defines your risk.
  • TP1 — the first target, where many traders take part of the position.
  • TP2 — the second target, for the remainder of the position.

Because the entry, the stop and the targets are all given, you can work out position size before you act, instead of after.

LevelWhat it answers
EntryWhere does the trade start?
Stop lossHow much am I risking if I am wrong?
TP1Where might I take a first piece off?
TP2Where is the fuller move expected to reach?
💡
Risk first, entry second The stop is the most important number in the signal. It tells you the idea is wrong before your account has to. Always know it before you size a position.

How the analysis engine decides

The engine is deliberately multi-factor. No single indicator gets the final vote. Instead the app layers several kinds of evidence and checks whether they agree:

📈 Trend and structure Market structure, order blocks, fair value gaps and liquidity shape where a move is likely to start.
⚡ Momentum Momentum, MACD and RSI help show whether a move is gathering strength or fading.
🎚️ Volatility bands Bollinger Bands and candlestick behaviour give context for how far a move might stretch.
🔁 Multi-timeframe check The setup is compared across timeframes, plus Fibonacci levels for confluence.

When the evidence lines up, you get a direction with levels. When it does not, you get a HOLD — which is the engine declining to invent an edge.

Crypto signals — quick answers

What exactly is a crypto signal?

It is a defined trade idea: a pair, a direction, an entry price, a stop loss and one or more targets. CPE Trade Pro adds the analysis behind each one so you can judge it rather than just follow it.

How many pairs are covered?

590+ combined crypto pairs, gathered from Binance Spot and USDT perpetual listings. The exact number changes as exchanges add and remove symbols.

Is HOLD a missed signal?

No. HOLD means the engine found no clean setup. Waiting is a decision, and often a profitable one, because it keeps you out of low-quality trades.

Can I short a coin on Spot?

Spot is long-only in the app. Shorting requires futures or a supported Forex workflow. Leverage can magnify losses.

Do the DEMO trades use real money?

No. DEMO uses a $10 paper stake per eligible signal and places no real orders. It is for practice on live market data.
